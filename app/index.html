Governance Lifecycle

How community proposals move from initiation to immutable execution on BotChain

STAGE 01

Proposal Initiation

Any member with at least 100 VOX submits a structured proposal with quorum target, voting window, and optional executable payload.

STAGE 02

Cryptographic Snapshot

Snapshot locks voting power at proposal block minus 1. ERC20Votes checkpoints render same-block flash loans powerless.

STAGE 03

Direct On-Chain Voting

Token holders vote FOR, AGAINST, or ABSTAIN with optional on-chain rationale stored directly in smart contract state.

STAGE 04

Autonomous Execution

Passed proposals meeting quorum execute treasury disbursements and smart contract calls trustlessly without intermediaries.
